For The Most Special Person ❤️

Pata hai… kuch log hamari life mein bas aate hain, aur kuch log hamari life ka ek khoobsurat hissa ban jaate hain. Tum mere liye wahi ho. ❤️


Tum meri sabse pyaari cheezon mein se ek ho, aur mere dil ke bahut, bahut kareeb ho. Shayad main har baar words mein explain nahi kar pata, lekin jo feelings mere dil mein tumhare liye hain, woh mere liye bahut special hain.


Aur please… tum chhoti-chhoti baaton par mujhse naraz mat hua karo. 🥺 Tumhari narazgi mujhe andar se bahut hurt karti hai. Agar kabhi mujhse koi galti ho jaaye, toh mujhse baat kar lena, daant lena, lekin mujhse door mat jaana.


Kabhi kabhi main sochta hoon… agar main chala gaya toh tum kya karogi? Kaun tumhe itna pareshan karega? Kaun tumhe baar-baar manayega? Kaun tumhari chhoti-chhoti baaton ko notice karega? Aur kaun tumhari smile dekhne ke liye itna pagal hoga? ❤️


I know you love me a lot… aur sach bolun toh, I love you so, so, so much too. Shayad jitna main keh pata hoon, usse kahin zyada.


Tumhare saath bitaya hua har little moment, har conversation, har smile, aur har chhoti si nok-jhok… mere liye special hai.


Main bas itna chahta hoon ki chahe life mein kitni bhi problems aayein, hum ek dusre ka haath kabhi na chhodein. Naraz ho jaana, lad lena, par ek dusre ko khona mat. 🥺❤️

"You are not just someone I love…
You are someone I never want to lose." ❤️
Forever yours,
Sahil Yadav ❤️
